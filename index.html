Bring a page. Follow a wonder.

Whykeeper helps a parent and child understand schoolwork and explore the objects, signs and stories they notice together.

From the page to a practice question

Photograph a short passage, pick an image, choose a page from a PDF or type an exercise. Crop the exercise, confirm AI processing and choose from the detected exercises. Get an explanation, worked examples and practice, then ask the AI to explain further. Grown-ups can also prepare a worksheet and answer key.

Explore what caught your eye

Photograph an object, a sign, a note or an advert. Choose a discovery and explore it through explanations, visuals, questions and safe activities. Newspaper and advert lessons help children distinguish claims, opinions and evidence. The AI cannot verify live news or guarantee every identification.

Return to a tricky idea

Save a passage on the device to revisit it. For revision, choose up to four saved pages to study together in one practice exam. For a break from the book, the illustrated field guide offers narrated investigations of everyday objects.
