Help for grown-ups

For help with Whykeeper, email contact@idealailabs.com. Include your device model, app version and the step where the problem happens. Please leave out children's names, recordings and pictures.

Why did the app ask me to choose?

Picture recognition can be uncertain. Use a clear image of one object and choose a matching subject. A family can also start from the illustrated lessons.

I cannot hear narration

Turn up media volume and check the English text-to-speech voice installed in your device’s accessibility or speech settings. Download an offline voice using the device’s settings if needed.

Camera or gallery access

Use system settings to review Whykeeper’s camera permission. You can start with an illustrated lesson while the camera is unavailable.
