Help for grown-ups

For help, email contact@idealailabs.com with your device model, app version and the step that needs attention. Please leave out your child’s name, schoolwork and photos.

How do I start with a schoolbook?

Choose a passage or a single exercise. Use the camera, Photos or an image file. Choose the page to read when you pick a PDF, or type in the exercise. Keep the page flat and well lit. Crop out school headers and personal details, confirm cloud processing, and select the exercise the AI identifies. You can select reading level and English or Arabic responses.

Which output should we use?

Try an explanation when an idea is new, guided practice when your child is ready to try, flashcards for quick recall or an exam for independent review. Print a worksheet through the grown-up control if your child prefers paper.

What if the answer seems wrong?

Compare the selected exercise with the book, especially digits, fractions and diagrams. Ask a follow-up or try a closer crop. AI can make mistakes; verify important answers with the teacher or textbook. A network error keeps your page available to retry.

Where is a saved page?

Choose to save a page when you want to revisit it. Saved school material stays on the device and can be removed there. Select up to four saved pages for mixed revision and choose how many questions to include.

How does speech work?

Open a sample activity to hear its included recordings. For other school text, Whykeeper uses an installed offline voice for the lesson language where the device provides one. Turn up media volume and check your device’s voice and audio output settings. The separate field-guide recordings play even when an offline system voice is unavailable.
