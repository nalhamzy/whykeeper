Privacy for families

Whykeeper helps a parent and child learn from schoolwork and everyday pictures. Its AI features use an online service; version 1.5 adds discovery from objects, signs, notes, newspapers and adverts, and version 1.5.1 lets a grown-up report an AI answer. The separate curated examples and field guide can still work on the device.

Before sending a picture or text

A grown-up chooses a learning picture or page, crops it and confirms processing. Remove children's names, school identifiers, contact details, faces, grades and any other identifying details. Keep only the object, text or exercise needed for the chosen discovery. Image metadata is removed. No image is sent to AI before confirmation.

AI study processing

In version 1.3.1 and later, your confirmed crop or typed learning material, chosen reading level/language, follow-up questions and answers are sent over HTTPS to Whykeeper's Google Cloud service and Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Qwen) to generate teaching material and feedback. Version 1.3.0 uses Anthropic through its separate service. The provider named in your installed app's confirmation is the provider used; there is no automatic transfer to the other provider.

Our service does not save or log page images, source text, questions, answers or generated lessons. Alibaba Cloud's standard policy permits retention of prompts and responses for up to 30 days, with legal and security exceptions. We do not claim zero retention. See Alibaba Cloud's retention policy and, for version 1.3.0, Anthropic's privacy information. Please do not include personal information in questions.

Reporting an AI answer

If an answer looks wrong or unsuitable, a grown-up can tap "Report this answer". The app then sends the answer text, the reason chosen, an optional note, the lesson title, study mode, language and app version to Whykeeper's Google Cloud service. No photo or source page is sent with a report. Reports are stored in Google Cloud Firestore with a one-way hashed study identifier, never a name or email, so we can review and fix problems. They are deleted automatically after 180 days. Please do not write personal details in the note.

Study connection and service protection

Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous study identity, without a name, email or password. A refresh credential remains on your device. The service checks its ID token and keeps hashed identifiers, request counts and expiry timestamps to limit abuse and cost. Counter records expire after two days subject to scheduled cleanup. Google Cloud and Firebase process ordinary connection and operational metadata, including IP addresses and authentication/security events. These are not used for advertising or learning profiles.

Saved material and deletion

Settings > Delete connection deletes the anonymous study identity from Firebase Authentication and removes its credential from your device. The AI source and current lesson are held for the session. Existing saved school pages, teacher notes, field-guide bookmarks and sound settings stay on your device and can be removed there. Removing Whykeeper or clearing its app data removes local records and the local study credential. Original photos and printed/exported documents remain wherever you saved them. To ask us to delete reports sooner, or for any other privacy request, contact contact@idealailabs.com.

Voice and printing

Read-aloud uses an available installed offline voice. Sample/field-guide narration also uses bundled recordings. Whykeeper does not record your child's voice. An adult's print action hands a document to the system destination selected; that service has its own privacy practices.

Whykeeper Plus (subscription)

From version 1.6.0, Whykeeper includes a number of AI lessons each day at no charge, and an optional subscription, Whykeeper Plus, that removes the daily limit. Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play with the account on your device. Apple or Google processes the payment; Whykeeper never receives your name, card or billing details. The app keeps a record on your device that a subscription is active and how many lessons were started today. This record is not sent to us and is not linked to the anonymous study identity. You can cancel at any time in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Restore Purchases in the app brings an existing subscription back on a new device.

No advertising or tracking

There are no ads, third-party analytics or social feeds. We do not sell information or use it for advertising.

Support and this website

We use an adult's support email to answer their request. Please omit children's personal details. GitHub Pages hosts this website and processes ordinary hosting connection information. There are no advertising or analytics scripts on this site.
