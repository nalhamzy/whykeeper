Privacy for families

Whykeeper helps a parent teach a child from schoolwork. From version 1.3, its AI schoolbook features use an online service. The separate curated examples and field guide can still work on the device.

Before sending a page

A grown-up chooses a page, crops it and confirms processing. Remove children's names, school identifiers, contact details, faces, grades and any other identifying details. Keep only the exercise and diagrams needed to understand it. Image metadata is removed. No image is sent to AI before confirmation.

AI study processing

Your confirmed crop or typed exercise, chosen reading level/language, follow-up questions and answers are sent over HTTPS to Whykeeper's Google Cloud service and Anthropic to generate teaching material and feedback. Our service does not save or log page images, source text, questions, answers or generated lessons. Anthropic processes requests under its commercial API terms and may retain inputs and outputs for a limited period, with exceptions for safety and legal requirements. This is not a zero-retention service. See Anthropic's privacy information. Please do not include personal information in questions.

Study connection and service protection

Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous study identity, without a name, email or password. A refresh credential remains on your device. The service checks its ID token and keeps hashed identifiers, request counts and expiry timestamps to limit abuse and cost. Counter records expire after two days subject to scheduled cleanup. Google Cloud and Firebase process ordinary connection and operational metadata, including IP addresses and authentication/security events. These are not used for advertising or learning profiles.

Saved material and deletion

The AI source and current lesson are held for the session. Existing saved school pages, teacher notes, field-guide bookmarks and sound settings stay on your device and can be removed there. Removing Whykeeper or clearing its app data removes local records and the local study credential. Original photos and printed/exported documents remain wherever you saved them. For a privacy request, contact contact@idealailabs.com.

Voice and printing

Read-aloud uses an available installed offline voice. Sample/field-guide narration also uses bundled recordings. Whykeeper does not record your child's voice. An adult's print action hands a document to the system destination selected; that service has its own privacy practices.

No advertising or tracking

There are no ads, third-party analytics, social feeds or purchases in this version. We do not sell information or use it for advertising.

Support and this website

We use an adult's support email to answer their request. Please omit children's personal details. GitHub Pages hosts this website and processes ordinary hosting connection information. There are no advertising or analytics scripts on this site.
