Privacy for families

Ideal Intelligence develops Whykeeper for children ages 6–8 and their grown-ups. The app processes picture matching and lessons on your device.

Photos and files

The app can use the system camera or a picture you choose from Photos or Files. The app reads that picture locally to suggest a supported subject. Whykeeper does not upload photos, camera images, filenames, or picture metadata to Ideal Intelligence or an AI provider. The saved field book records lesson choices. Your original photo or file remains under your control.

Device settings and voice

The app stores preferences and saved lesson IDs on your device. English narration is included in the app and plays locally. Lessons, definitions, observations, questions and answer explanations play from bundled recordings. The app does not record speech or access the microphone for narration.

Accounts and tracking

Whykeeper has no user accounts, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics service or social feed. We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or collect learning activity for profiling.

Your choices

You can revoke camera or photo access in device settings. Remove the app or clear its app data to erase its local records. Deleting a record in Whykeeper does not delete originals in Photos or Files.

Support and this website

If an adult emails us, we receive the email address and whatever the adult includes. We use it to respond to the request. Please do not send a child’s name, voice, photograph or other identifying details. To request removal of a support message, email contact@idealailabs.com.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and can process ordinary connection information, including IP addresses. These pages include no advertising or analytics scripts. The website is separate from the app’s local learning activities.
