Privacy for families

Ideal Intelligence develops Whykeeper for children ages 6–8 and their grown-ups. Your device reads school pages, builds study activities and matches field-guide pictures.

School pages and pictures

Whykeeper can use the system camera, an image or PDF you select from your device, or text you type. The app processes the page locally. It does not upload the image, extracted text, your edits, your answers or image metadata to us or an AI service. The system picker may create a temporary local image copy. Your original file remains in the location you chose it from.

Saved material

Choosing to save a school page stores its study text, selected subject and any teacher answer notes you entered on this device. You can remove saved pages individually. Your field-guide bookmarks store topic choices. Removing Whykeeper or clearing its app data removes the local records. Original pictures and any documents you printed or saved elsewhere remain under your control.

Voice and printing

Read-aloud for school material uses an available offline English voice on the device. The sample study activities also include recordings. The field guide plays included recordings. Neither feature records a child's voice. When an adult opens the system print dialog, the worksheet or answer key goes to the destination the adult chooses. A print service or cloud destination may use its own data practices.

Accounts and tracking

The app has no accounts, ads, social feed, purchases or analytics service. We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising or collect learning activity for profiling.

Support and website hosting

When an adult emails support, we receive the address and the information included. We use them to answer the request. Please leave out children’s names, schoolwork and photographs. To request removal of a support message, contact contact@idealailabs.com.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process ordinary connection information, including IP addresses. These pages contain no advertising or analytics scripts.
